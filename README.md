# Shotgun Metagenomic Analysis in Pediatric With Acute Diarrhea
Acute diarrhea in children is often associated with changes in the gut microbiome and an increased prevalence of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) genes. Studies have shown that diarrheagenic Escherichia coli (DEC) strains are common pathogens and often exhibit resistance to multiple antibiotics, including cephalosporins and trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole. This study characterizes taxonomic diversity, metabolic pathways, and antimicrobial resistance (AMR) genes in pediatric gut microbiomes using shotgun metagenomic sequencing.
